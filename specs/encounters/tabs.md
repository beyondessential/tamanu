# Encounter view tabs

The encounter view organises clinical information into a row of tabs (e.g. Notes, Vitals, Medication). Staff can reorder the tabs by dragging them, and can select a tab by clicking it.

## Behaviour

- [ ] Clicking a tab selects it and shows its contents, regardless of whether the tab bar also supports reordering
- [ ] Dragging a tab moves it to a new position among the other tabs
- [ ] The tab shows a standard pointer cursor on hover, since selecting a tab is its primary action; it does not show a grab/grabbing cursor
