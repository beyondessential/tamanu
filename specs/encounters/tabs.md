# Encounter view tabs

Applies to the row of tabs the encounter view organises clinical information into (Notes, Vitals, Medication and the rest), and to selecting and reordering them.

## Behaviour

- Clicking a tab selects it and shows its contents, regardless of whether the tab bar also supports reordering
- Dragging a tab moves it to a new position among the other tabs
- The tab shows a standard pointer cursor on hover; it does not show a grab/grabbing cursor

> [!NOTE]
> The pointer cursor follows from selecting a tab being its primary action, with reordering secondary to it.
