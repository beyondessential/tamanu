# Selected patient on mobile

Applies to the one patient the mobile app treats as selected: how a patient becomes selected, what the selection drives, when it is cleared, and how long it lasts.

## Selecting a patient

- At most one patient is selected on a device at a time.
- A patient becomes selected when the user opens them from the patient search results, from the recently viewed list in search, or from the recently viewed tiles on the home screen.
- Registering a new patient selects that patient.
- Selecting a patient replaces whichever patient was selected before.

## What the selection drives

- The Patient tab shows the selected patient's home screen, and shows patient search when no patient is selected.
- The patient screens reached from the patient's home (vitals, vaccines, referrals, programs, lab requests, patient details and the rest) all act on the selected patient.
- Every screen showing the selected patient updates together when the selection changes.

## Keeping the selection current

- Saving edits to the selected patient's details updates the selection with the saved record.
- Submitting a form, vitals reading or referral for the selected patient reloads the selection from the device's database, so fields the submission wrote back to the patient show straight away.
- Submitting for a patient other than the selected one leaves the selection as it is.

## Recently viewed

- Selecting a patient puts them at the top of the device's recently viewed patients.
- A patient already in the list moves to the top instead of appearing twice.
- The list keeps the 20 most recently selected patients.
- The recently viewed tiles and list update as soon as a patient is selected.

## Clearing the selection

- Going back from the patient's home screen clears the selection.
- Opening patient search from the home screen, and returning home from patient search, each clear the selection.

## How long a selection lasts

- A selected patient stays selected until another patient is selected or the selection is cleared.
- The selection survives the app closing and reopening, and the app updating to a newer release.
- The selection stays through signing out and signing in again, whichever user signs in.
