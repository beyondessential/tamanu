# Billing information section on patient details

## Decisions

- Billing information is a new section on the patient details form, below Location information. It holds insurer and insurance policy number (moved from Identification information), insurance plan and patient type (moved from Personal information), plus annual income, family size, payer one and payer two
- Payer one and payer two link to other patient records, stored and searched the same way as mother and father (a patient reference on the patient's additional data, chosen through the patient suggester)
- Annual income and family size are new fields on the patient record, entered as plain numbers. They are not tied to the invoice discount assessment, whose income bands come from the sliding fee scale setting and depend on family size
- Adoption number and marriage licence are new text fields at the end of Identification information
