In-app update: installed when staff choose

Tamanu downloads its update in the background while staff keep working offline. It only installs when someone taps to install, because installing closes the app and Android can't reopen it.

Logged out, and new devices

Logging out means someone has finished, so Tamanu installs the update and closes by itself. A new device has nothing to log in to until it updates.
