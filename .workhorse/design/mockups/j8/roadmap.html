Roadmap

A best-estimate view of major developments in Tamanu, from recently released to planned. Move along the timeline to see each release.

Released Planned
V2.24 · Jan 25 Released
  • Improved scheduling and appointments module, including ability to book rooms and other locations
  • Clinician dashboard, new landing experience for users
View details
V2.40 · Sept 25 Released
  • Medication charts, including medication administration record and improvements to the 'ongoing medications' module
  • Desktop charting to support speciality-specific documentation as well as standard clinical assessments such as wound management and pain assessments
  • Inclusion of 'Form' features into the procedures workflow allowing virtually any data to be collected against a procedure in configurable form fields
View details
Jan – Mar 2026 Planned
  • Enhancements to the invoicing module to support automated invoice generation, and more flexible and adaptable pricing structures
  • Medication dispensing, ability to send medication orders to pharmacy and track dispensing
  • Expansion of FHIR API to send prescriptions to mSupply
  • Expansion of FHIR API to receive lab results directly to Tamanu from SENAITE
View details
V2.34 · Jun 25 Released
  • Enhancements to auditability capabilities
  • Enhancements to program registries to capture condition categories
  • Intelligent patient duplicate detection on new patient creation
View details
Oct – Dec 2025 Planned
  • Secure Patient Portal pulling all of a patient's key health information into one place, additionally supports patient form completion and reminders
  • Enhancements to location bookings to support a daily view and allocation of surgical lists
  • Ability to link a chart to a program registry enabling viewing chart entries across encounters
  • Enhancements to streamline the encounter management workflow
View details
Apr – Jun 2026 Planned
  • Customisable Early Warning Score (EWS) for identification of patients at risk of clinical deterioration
  • Enhancements to program forms to edit a program response
  • Admin panel enhancements to streamline Tamanu configuration including an interface to manage permissions
View details

Disclaimer. The presented roadmap is a dynamic plan intended to communicate the 'best estimate' for planned features in Tamanu and is subject to change. It focuses on major developments including new features and major enhancements — it does not include all new features, performance improvements, regular maintenance or bug fixes.

Please continue to liaise with your respective project managers or contact support@tamanu.io for specific release plans and dates.
