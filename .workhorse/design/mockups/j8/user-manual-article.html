Sending medications to pharmacy

How to send prescribed medications to pharmacy at prescribing and at discharge.

Medications can be sent to pharmacy at the two points where prescribing happens: when writing a new prescription, and when discharging a patient. Sending a medication creates a pharmacy order so it can be dispensed and tracked.

Before you start

The pharmacy fields appear only where the pharmacy order setting is enabled for your facility. If you do not see them, ask your administrator.

Sending when prescribing

When you write a new prescription, you can send it to pharmacy in the same step.

  1. Open the patient's encounter and select New prescription.
  2. Complete the medication details as usual.
  3. Turn on Send to pharmacy. It is off by default.
  4. Enter a Dispensing quantity, then save the prescription.

Sending at discharge

At discharge, the patient's encounter medications are ticked to send by default, so routine discharge medications go to pharmacy without extra steps.

  • The Discharge modal shows a Send to pharmacy column, ticked for encounter medications and unticked for other ongoing medications.
  • Stock on hand, when a request was last sent, and whether it is still active or already dispensed are shown while you decide.
  • Dispensing quantity must be filled in for each medication being sent.

Tracking a sent medication

Once sent, a medication appears in the pharmacy's queue. The prescription record shows its status as it moves from ordered to dispensed.
