Tamanu v2.64 release notes

Tamanu v2.64 adds hourly and half-hourly medication frequencies across prescribing and the medication administration record, and makes Open mSupply the authority on stock on hand where that integration is live. It also carries substantial performance work on long-stay encounters and on Tamanu Mobile. Two changes alter existing behaviour on upgrade, so the upgrade notes below are worth reading in full.

🌟Major Features and Changes

Tamanu Desktop

Hourly and half-hourly medication frequencies

The medication frequency list gains 'Hourly' and 'Half-hourly', supporting closely monitored regimes. The medication administration record has been reworked to hold the resulting volume of doses.

Key features

  • Two new frequencies - 'Hourly' and 'Half-hourly' can be selected when prescribing, and are found by the usual shorthand searches. Both are switched on by default, and either can be turned off in the frequency list.
  • Individual dose slots on the record - The medication administration record keeps its twelve two-hour columns, and each column is now divided into separate dose slots: two for an hourly medication, four for a half-hourly one. Each slot is recorded against individually, with its own status and tooltip, so every dose is captured rather than one per column.
  • Simplified prescribing - The 'Medication administration schedule' section does not appear for these two frequencies, since their administration times are fixed. It returns if the frequency is changed to any other option.
  • Accessibility and presentation - The record is now navigable by keyboard and readable by screen readers, which announce the current time slot. Column widths, headings, icons and empty states have been brought into line with the design.

🔧System Enhancements

Open mSupply stock on hand

Where a deployment receives stock data from Open mSupply, mSupply can now be set as the authority on stock on hand. Stock levels carried in the drug reference data import are then ignored for that facility, so a routine re-import no longer overwrites live stock figures, and a sync from central can no longer overwrite them either. Teams no longer need to strip the stock columns out of the spreadsheet before every import, and prescribers no longer see incorrect stock levels while waiting for the next mSupply sync.

The single mSupply switch is replaced by two independent ones, so pushing dispensed medications to mSupply and taking stock on hand from mSupply can be turned on separately per facility. Existing settings are converted automatically on upgrade.

  • Preselect send to pharmacy on discharge - Whether encounter medications arrive with 'Send to pharmacy' already ticked on the discharge modal is now a facility-level choice, through the new 'Preselect send to pharmacy when preparing discharge' setting. Medications under 'Other ongoing medications' are never preselected, and the standard send to pharmacy workflow is unchanged.
  • Performance on high-volume encounters - The vitals and charts tables load reliably on long-stay admissions with very large numbers of recorded observations, where they could previously hang until server load dropped. Patient lab results and survey response history load faster on the same deployments, and routine database housekeeping is tuned so it keeps up on busy facility servers.
  • Encounter diagnosis on shared medication orders - Medication orders shared with external systems carry the encounter's diagnoses as the clinical reason for the order, so receiving pharmacy and national systems get the indication without a separate lookup. Diagnoses marked disproven or entered in error are excluded.

🐛Tweaks and Bug Fixes

Desktop

  • Vaccinations - The include not given filter is kept when a vaccine record is edited.
  • Dates - Date defaults and limits follow the facility timezone rather than the server's.

Mobile

  • Performance - A broad performance pass covering screen rendering, how data is loaded and cached, database indexes, and a larger cache for incremental sync. Screens are more responsive and sync completes faster, particularly on lower-end devices.
  • Fixes - The vitals 'Add' tab submits correctly after a previous submission, the last sync pulled count is accurate, a race condition during batch record inserts is resolved, and a flashing indicator on overflowing content is removed. A synced prescription with no administration times no longer fails when its first administration date is generated.

System

  • Sync - Duplicate patient invoice insurance plan records are resolved, and merged patients are correctly flagged so their sync records rebuild.

⚠️Critical Upgrade Notes

Required

Two changes alter existing behaviour on upgrade: medications are no longer preselected for pharmacy on discharge, and the mSupply integration switch has been replaced. Both are covered below.

Send to pharmacy on discharge

  • Encounter medications are no longer selected for 'Send to pharmacy' automatically on the discharge modal. If your teams rely on that, turn on 'Preselect send to pharmacy when preparing discharge' for each facility to restore it.
  • If you take no action, staff will need to select medications individually at discharge.

Open mSupply integration

  • The single mSupply switch has been replaced by two: one for pushing dispensed medications, one for taking stock on hand from mSupply. Existing settings are converted automatically, so behaviour is preserved without administrator action.
  • Check both switches at each mSupply facility after upgrading, particularly any facility that has not synced recently, as the conversion reaches a facility through sync.
  • Administrators who previously set the old switch will need to use the two new ones from now on.

Medication frequencies

  • 'Hourly' and 'Half-hourly' are enabled by default and will appear in the frequency list as soon as you upgrade. Disable them in the frequency list if your deployment does not want them offered.
  • Brief prescribing and nursing staff that the administration schedule section does not appear for these two frequencies, and that their administration times are fixed rather than configurable.

Shared medication orders

  • Encounter diagnoses are added to outbound medication orders for all deployments, with no opt-in. The upgrade includes a database change, and existing medication orders are regenerated, so allow for additional processing time in the upgrade window.

Upgrade Steps and Recommended Testing

Required

Send to pharmacy on discharge

  • Open the discharge modal for a patient with encounter medications and confirm the preselection matches what you decided, whether on or off.

Open mSupply integration

  • Confirm both new switches carry the values you expect at every mSupply facility.
  • Re-import your drug reference data and confirm stock levels from mSupply are retained.

Medication frequencies

  • Prescribe a medication at each new frequency and confirm the administration schedule section is hidden, then change the frequency and confirm it returns.
  • Open the medication administration record for an hourly medication, confirm each column shows separate dose slots, and record a dose against an individual slot.
Optional

Shared medication orders

  • If you exchange medication orders with an external system, confirm a new order carries the encounter diagnosis as expected.

General

  • Open the vitals and charts tables on your longest-running admissions and confirm they load rather than hang.
