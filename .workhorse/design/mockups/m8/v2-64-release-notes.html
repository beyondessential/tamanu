Tamanu v2.64 Released 10-09-2026

Tamanu v2.64 release notes

Tamanu v2.64 is a moderate release. It adds hourly and half-hourly medication frequencies across prescribing and the medication administration record, makes Open mSupply the source of truth for stock on hand where that integration is live, and carries substantial performance work on long-stay encounters and on Tamanu Mobile.

🌟Major Features and Changes

Tamanu Desktop

Hourly and half-hourly medication frequencies

The medication frequency list gains 'Hourly' and 'Half-hourly' options, supporting closely monitored regimes that previously had to be recorded as a series of separate orders. Both prescribing and the medication administration record accommodate the higher volume of doses these frequencies produce.

Key features

  • New frequency options - 'Hourly' and 'Half-hourly' can be selected when prescribing a medication, alongside the existing frequencies.
  • Simplified prescribing - The 'Medication administration schedule' section is hidden in the 'New prescription' modal when either frequency is selected, and reappears if the frequency is changed to any other option.
  • Medication administration record - The record displays hourly and half-hourly medications so that a full day of doses remains readable, rather than overflowing the standard layout.
  • Configurable administration times - Default administration times can be set for the new frequencies through the admin panel settings, as for every other frequency.

🔧System Enhancements

Open mSupply stock on hand

Where a deployment receives stock data from Open mSupply, mSupply is now treated as the source of truth for stock on hand. Stock levels carried in the drug reference data import are ignored, so a routine drug reference data re-import no longer overwrites live stock levels. Teams no longer need to strip the stock columns out of the spreadsheet before every import, and prescribers no longer see incorrect stock levels while waiting for the next mSupply sync.

  • Preselect send to pharmacy on discharge - A new facility-level setting, 'Preselect send to pharmacy when preparing discharge', preselects the 'Send to pharmacy' checkbox for every medication under 'Encounter medications' on the discharge modal. It is off by default. Medications under 'Other ongoing medications' are never preselected, and the standard send to pharmacy workflow is unchanged.
  • Performance on high-volume encounters - The vitals and charts tables load reliably on long-stay admissions with very large numbers of recorded observations, where they could previously hang until server load dropped. Patient lab results and survey response history load faster on the same deployments, and routine database housekeeping is tuned so it keeps up on busy facility servers.
  • Encounter diagnosis on shared medication orders - Medication orders shared with external systems now carry the encounter diagnosis as the reason for the order, giving receiving systems the clinical context behind a prescription.

🐛Tweaks and Bug Fixes

Desktop

  • Medications - The medication administration record no longer fails for a drug recorded without a dosing unit. Printed medication labels use a dedicated print frame with a fixed page size and a wider dispensed label field. A medication discontinued from the discharge modal appears struck through, drugs without a matching reference record can be selected when prescribing, and a drug reference data export can be re-imported without error.
  • Labs - The interim results printout works before any result has been published, and long test category names no longer collide with test names on the printout.
  • Imaging - A completion time is recorded when an imaging request is completed.
  • Tasking and dashboard - New tasks are saved before the dashboard responds, bulk task deletion works reliably, dashboard tasks filter correctly by designation, and a single task change no longer refreshes every clinician's dashboard.
  • Patients and encounters - Fixed a freeze when opening certain encounters. Saving patient details now confirms success, longer patient issue notes can be recorded, and the encounter refreshes once a death is recorded.
  • Vaccinations - The include not given filter is kept when a vaccine record is edited.
  • Dates - Date defaults and limits follow the facility timezone rather than the server's.
  • Invoicing and surveys - Fixed an error when adding an invoice item, and a rare crash when the survey 'Complete' button is clicked.
  • AI summaries - Refinements to patient and encounter summary wording and presentation.

Mobile

  • Performance - A broad performance pass covering screen rendering, how data is loaded and cached, database indexes, and a larger cache for incremental sync. Screens are more responsive and sync completes faster, particularly on lower-end devices.
  • Fixes - The vitals 'Add' tab submits correctly after a previous submission, the last sync pulled count is accurate, a race condition during batch record inserts is resolved, and a flashing indicator on overflowing content is removed.

System

  • Sync - Duplicate patient invoice insurance plan records are resolved, and merged patients are correctly flagged so their sync records rebuild.
  • Integrations - Fixed an issue where part of a failed data exchange bundle could be saved instead of being rolled back in full.
  • Facility servers - Unrecognised clients are exempt from the client version check, so an unknown client is no longer blocked outright.

⚠️Critical Upgrade Notes

Required

Medication frequencies

  • Review the default administration times for 'Hourly' and 'Half-hourly' and set them to suit your deployment before staff begin prescribing with them.
  • Brief prescribing and nursing staff that the 'Medication administration schedule' section does not appear for these two frequencies, so they are not expecting to set one.
Optional

Open mSupply stock on hand

  • If you receive stock data from Open mSupply, no action is required. You can stop deleting the stock columns from the drug reference data spreadsheet before re-importing it.
  • If you set stock on hand manually and do not use mSupply, nothing changes for you.

Preselect send to pharmacy on discharge

  • This setting is off by default. Decide whether preselecting suits your discharge workflow and enable it per facility if so.
  • If you leave it off, the discharge modal behaves exactly as it does today.

Upgrade Steps and Recommended Testing

Required

Medication frequencies

  • Prescribe a medication with each new frequency and confirm the administration schedule section is hidden, then change the frequency and confirm it returns.
  • Open the medication administration record for an hourly medication and confirm a full day of doses is readable and can be recorded against.
Optional

Open mSupply stock on hand

  • Re-import your drug reference data and confirm stock levels from mSupply are retained.

Preselect send to pharmacy on discharge

  • If enabling, confirm encounter medications are preselected on the discharge modal and other ongoing medications are not.

General

  • Open the vitals and charts tables on your longest-running admissions and confirm they load rather than hang.
  • Verify that existing workflows continue to function as expected.
