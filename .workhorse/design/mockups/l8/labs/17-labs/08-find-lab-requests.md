The **Labs** listings show lab requests for every patient, so laboratory staff and clinicians can find
a request without opening each patient's record. **Active requests** lists requests still in progress,
and **Finalised** lists requests that are published, invalidated or rejected.

# Steps

1. In the menu on the left, select **Labs**, then **Active requests** or **Finalised**.

   **[Screenshot: the Labs menu open, showing Active requests and Finalised]**

2. To narrow the list, fill in any of the search fields, such as the patient's name, **Request ID**
   or **Test category**, then select **Search**. More fields, such as **Department** and **Area**, are
   in the advanced search. On **Finalised**, **Status** narrows the list to published, invalidated
   or rejected requests.

   **[Screenshot: the Active lab requests listing, searched for one patient]**

3. Select a request to open it.

When the laboratory rejects a sample, the requesting clinician gets a notification on their
**Dashboard**. Selecting it opens the rejected request.
