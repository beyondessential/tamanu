You can read a request's results on the lab request itself as soon as they are entered. Once a request
is published, its results also appear on the patient's **Results** tab, alongside their earlier
results.

# Before you start

You need permission to view lab results. If you believe you should be able to view them, contact your
system administrator.

# Steps

## On the lab request

1. On the **Labs** tab of the patient's encounter, select the request. The results table lists each
   test with its **Result**, **Units**, **Reference** range, **Method**, **Lab officer**,
   **Verification** and **Completed** date.
2. Check for highlighted results. A result outside its reference range is highlighted, and hovering
   over it shows the limit it is outside. A value changed after it was first entered has a faded
   asterisk beside it, and **\* Edited entry** appears below the table.

   **[Screenshot: a lab request's results table with an out-of-range result highlighted]**

3. To save the results as a file, select **Download** below the table.

## On the patient's record

1. On the patient's record, select the **Results** tab.
2. Choose a **Test category** or a **Test panel**. The table shows each test's **Test type** and
   **Normal range**, with a column of results for each date. Results outside the normal range are
   highlighted.
