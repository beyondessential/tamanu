Log in to Tamanu Desktop with your email address and password, and log out when you have finished. Start from your web browser.

Before you start

You need your site's Tamanu address, and the email address and password for your Tamanu account. Your system administrator gives you these.

Log in

  1. Open your site's Tamanu address in your web browser.

    The Log in screen opens. If you ticked Remember me last time, the heading says Welcome back and your email address is already filled in.


    The Log in screen.

  2. Enter your email address in Email and your password in Password.

  3. Tick Remember me if you want Tamanu to fill in your email address next time you log in on this computer.

    Leave it unticked to have Tamanu forget your email address.

  4. Select Log in.


    Enter your email address and password, then select Log in.

    If your email address or password is wrong, a message appears under the heading. It may tell you how many attempts you have left before you are locked out. If you are locked out, the message says how many minutes to wait before trying again. If you have forgotten your password, see Reset your password.

  5. If the Select facility screen opens, choose where you are working from Facility, then select Submit.

    This screen opens only when your account can work at more than one facility. Select Cancel to go back to the Log in screen.

    Tamanu opens, with the sidebar on the left of the screen.

Log out

Your site may also log you out after a period of inactivity. Before it does, the Login timeout window counts down the seconds left. Select Stay logged in to carry on working, or Log out to log out straight away.

  1. If the sidebar is collapsed, select the arrow at the top of the sidebar to expand it.

  2. Select Log out at the bottom of the sidebar, beside the version number.

    You cannot log out while you have a note open. Save or close the note first.


    Select Log out at the bottom of the sidebar.

    The Log in screen opens.

Set a new password for your Tamanu account if you have forgotten yours. Start from the Log in screen.

Before you start

You need access to the email inbox for your Tamanu account, because Tamanu sends a reset code there.

Steps

  1. Select Forgot password? under the Log in button.

    The Forgot password screen opens. If you ticked Remember me when you last logged in, your email address is already filled in.


    Enter your email address, then select Send reset code.

  2. Enter your email address in Email, then select Send reset code.

    The Reset password screen opens. If the email address belongs to a Tamanu account, an email with a reset code is sent to it. If no email arrives, contact your system administrator.

  3. Enter the code from the email in Reset code.

    If the code is wrong, Code incorrect appears under the field. To get a new code, select Resend reset code, which takes you back to the Forgot password screen.

  4. Enter your new password in New password, then enter it again in Confirm new password.

    Your new password must be at least five characters long, and the two fields must match.

  5. Select Reset password.


    Enter the reset code and your new password, then select Reset password.

    Password successfully reset appears. Select Back to login, then log in with your new password, as described in Log in and out.


    Select Back to login to log in with your new password.

Choose the language Tamanu shows its screens in. You can change it on the Log in screen, or from the sidebar once you have logged in. Tamanu remembers your choice in this web browser.

Before you start

You can change language only if your site has set Tamanu up in more than one language. If you cannot see Language on the Log in screen, or Change language in the sidebar menu, your site uses a single language. Contact your system administrator if you believe you should be able to change it.

On the Log in screen

  1. Select Language at the bottom right of the Log in screen.


    Select the language selector at the bottom right of the Log in screen.

  2. Choose your language.

    The screen changes to that language straight away.

After logging in

  1. If the sidebar is collapsed, select the arrow at the top of the sidebar to expand it.

  2. Select the menu button, shown as three dots, beside your name at the bottom of the sidebar.

    A menu opens.

  3. Select Change language.

    The Change language window opens.


    The Change language window.

  4. Choose your language from Language.

  5. Select Confirm.

    The window closes and Tamanu changes to that language.
