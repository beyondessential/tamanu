# 🌟 Major Features and Changes

## Tamanu Desktop

### _Encounter Fees_

Tamanu now applies encounter-driven fees automatically through a single fee engine and price list. A visit attracts an outpatient or emergency fee at the start of the encounter, an admission accrues a bed fee for each qualifying night, and facilities can choose to fold selected clinical item categories into an inpatient fee rather than billing them separately. Because every fee is priced through the existing price-list system, the same per-facility rate, age band, insurance eligibility and discount rules apply consistently across all fees.

**Key features**

- **Outpatient and emergency fees** - A fee is added once at the start of an encounter, with the amount varying by time of day - standard hours, after-hours, or weekend. The in-hours boundary is configurable per facility, and the emergency window can be set separately from the outpatient one.
- **Walk-in pharmacy fee** - A flat fee is charged for encounters created in the configured pharmacy department, keeping pharmacy dispensing on the same single price list as clinic visits.
- **Inpatient bed fees** - A nightly process charges each qualifying night at the rate for the patient's location, with the first night charged on admission. Bed fees are batched by location on the invoice rather than shown as one row per night.
- **Inpatient fee bundling** - Facilities can bundle selected item categories (such as imaging, lab, or medications) into the inpatient fee instead of invoicing them separately. This is configured per facility, and applies only to admissions.
- **Cashier control** - Automatically added fees behave like manual items - a cashier can adjust or remove them, for example on a public holiday, and a removed fee is not re-added on a later re-run or re-sync.

### _Medication Dispensing Quantity Autocalculation_

Tamanu can now automatically calculate the dispensing quantity when a prescription is created, using the medication's dispensing unit, frequency, and duration. This saves prescribers from working out quantities by hand and gives pharmacy a reliable figure to dispense against.

**Key features**

- **Automatic calculation** - The dispensing quantity is calculated as dispensing unit quantity × frequency × duration, with one month treated as 30 days and ongoing medications defaulting to a one-month supply.
- **Sensible exclusions** - Quantities are left blank for prescriptions where a calculation would be unreliable - variable doses, an 'as directed' frequency, a duration given in hours, or a missing duration on a non-ongoing medication.
- **Editable** - Calculated quantities can be edited, and a manual entry is respected from that point on. Modifying a prescription updates the quantity without changing the original prescription.
- **Configurable** - The feature is controlled by a 'Dispensing quantity autocalculation' setting under Medications, so a deployment can enable or disable it.

### _Modify Prescription When Dispensing_

Pharmacy staff can now modify a prescription at the point of dispensing to reflect what is actually available in stock, without changing the original prescription. This supports the common situation where pharmacy substitutes an equivalent strength or formulation, while keeping a clear record of what changed and why.

**Key features**

- **Modify prescription workflow** - A 'Modify prescription' option on the dispense medication modal opens a form to adjust the medication for dispensing purposes only. The original prescription is preserved.
- **Recorded and reviewable** - Modified medications are marked in the dispensed medication tables, and a 'View modify history' option shows the original and current prescription details side by side.
- **Reason capture** - A configurable 'Medication Dispense Modify Reason' reference data list records why a change was made, along with the user who made it.
- **Prescriber notification** - The original prescriber receives a pharmacy note notification so they are aware the prescription was modified during dispensing.

# 🔧 System Enhancements

### _Emergency Patients Board Enhancements_

The Emergency patients board gains search filters and clearer at-a-glance information, helping clinicians focus on the patients under their care.

**Key features**

- **Search filters** - Filter the board by patient ID, first name, last name, clinician, triage category, area, and location. Area must be selected before location, and filters are retained when navigating away from and back to the board.
- **Supervising clinician column** - A new sortable column shows the current supervising clinician.
- **Clearer tiles and columns** - 'Seen' tiles show the encounter type, the sex column displays 'M' and 'F', and default triage category colours have been refreshed while remaining configurable.

### _Encounter Notes Search_

A search bar has been added to the encounter notes view, letting clinicians quickly find notes rather than scrolling through the full history.

**Key features**

- **Free-text and field filters** - Search note body text, and filter by note type, author, and a date range. Filters update the table dynamically.
- **Consistent ordering** - Results stay in reverse chronological order, with the treatment plan pinned to the top when included.

# ⚠️ Critical Upgrade Notes

### Required

**_Encounter Fees_**

- Review the encounter fees feature and configure the facility-level fee windows (outpatient and emergency in-hours boundaries and the overnight bed-fee check time) before enabling invoicing.
- Set up the encounter fee, bed fee, and pharmacy fee products on your facility price list. Unpriced clinic and emergency fee products surface as $0 lines so misconfiguration stays visible; hide a product to suppress its fee.
- Where admissions should bundle clinical item categories into the inpatient fee, configure the bundled-category list per facility.

**Optional**

**_Medication Dispensing_**

- Review the dispensing quantity autocalculation feature and enable the 'Dispensing quantity autocalculation' setting under Medications if appropriate for your deployment.
- Configure the 'Medication Dispense Modify Reason' reference data list, and set up `MedicationDispense` write permissions for pharmacy staff who will modify prescriptions when dispensing.
