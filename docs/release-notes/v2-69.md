Tamanu&nbsp;v2.69 also carries the work of v2.68, which was not released separately. It introduces a
self check-in kiosk, syndromic surveillance with a new 'Diagnosis' tab on encounters, a redesigned
Patient Portal where patients keep their own details up to date, receipt printing for invoices, and a
'Recently discharged patients' view. It also tightens security across the servers, which changes
what each server needs configured before it will start.

---

# 🌟 Major features and changes

## Tamanu Desktop

### Self check-in kiosk

The self check-in kiosk runs on a tablet at a facility's reception, where patients check themselves
in for an appointment booked for today. A patient finds their appointment, confirms who they are,
completes the forms their appointment needs, and checks in, which marks the appointment 'Arrived'.
This takes routine check-in and form filling off reception staff, and the forms are completed by the
patient rather than transcribed.

**Key features**

- **Finding the appointment** - The patient enters their last name and date of birth. Outpatient
  appointments and location bookings for today at the facility are both found, and a patient with
  no matching appointment is directed to reception.
- **Confirming identity** - A single match asks 'Is this you?'. Where several patients match, each
  is shown with its appointment time and a partly hidden patient ID, and nothing more of anyone's
  record reaches the tablet until the patient has chosen.
- **Forms before check-in** - Each facility chooses which forms are completed for each appointment
  type and location booking type. Patients complete them in any order, questions that fill from the
  patient record show the existing value, and every form must be complete before checking in.
- **Checking in** - Checking in saves the forms to the patient's record and marks the appointment
  'Arrived'. A patient already checked in, including by reception while they were filling in their
  forms, is told so and asked to take a seat.
- **Language and accessibility** - Patients can choose from the deployment's languages, enlarge the
  text and switch to high contrast. These reset for the next patient.
- **Inactivity** - A check-in left idle asks 'Are you still there?' and then clears itself, so the
  next patient starts afresh. Facilities can set how long the kiosk waits.
- **Opening the kiosk** - Where the kiosk is on, 'Self check-in kiosk' under Facility admin in the
  sidebar opens it in a new browser tab.

**Security considerations**

The kiosk is limited to the facility the tablet is signed in to. It has its own permission, so the
tablet's account can be given permission to run the kiosk and nothing else, showing no patient
records if someone leaves the kiosk for the rest of Tamanu. Every patient record the kiosk shows is
recorded in the access audit.

**Keen to implement the self check-in kiosk?**

Please reach out to your Tamanu system administrator/project manager to discuss implementation
details.

### Syndromic surveillance and the 'Diagnosis' tab

Syndromic surveillance records the symptoms a patient presents with, independent of a formal
diagnosis, so a deployment can watch for patterns across its patient population. Each deployment
designs its own syndromic surveillance form, and clinicians complete it once per encounter from the
encounter's new 'Diagnosis' tab or while discharging.

**Key features**

- **'Diagnosis' tab** - An encounter's diagnoses are listed on their own 'Diagnosis' tab, showing
  each diagnosis's type, date, clinician and certainty, with 'Add diagnosis' beside them. Primary
  diagnoses are shown above the encounter's tabs, and the encounter's details panel shows its less
  used fields under 'Show more'.
- **Completing the form** - While the encounter is open, a 'Syndromic surveillance' action on the
  'Diagnosis' tab opens the form. Once completed, the tab shows 'Syndromic surveillance: Completed',
  and the response can be viewed or edited from there or from 'Forms'.
- **At discharge** - The discharge form includes the syndromic surveillance form until it has been
  completed for the encounter. Finalising a discharge with answers that have not been submitted
  warns that they will be lost.
- **Required at discharge** - A deployment can require syndromic surveillance before a discharge can
  be finalised. Encounters the system discharges automatically are not held up.
- **One response per encounter** - The form is offered only on the 'Diagnosis' tab and the discharge
  form, and is left out of the forms users choose from elsewhere, on desktop and mobile.
- **Permissions** - The 'Diagnosis' tab follows the form's usual permissions. Completing the form at
  discharge needs only the permissions to discharge.

### Receipt printing for invoices

Facilities that print at a point-of-sale printer can print invoices as receipts on 80 mm thermal
roll paper, in place of the full A4 page. Each facility chooses its layout, and both layouts print
the same figures.

**Key features**

- **Receipt layout** - A continuous strip as long as its content, with the facility's letterhead,
  the patient's name, date of birth and patient ID, the invoice number and date, each item with its
  quantity and total, the invoice totals and the patient total due.
- **Per-facility layout** - Each facility prints in A4 or receipt layout. A4 is the default.
- **Translated labels** - Every label on the receipt can be translated, and patient field labels
  follow the deployment's own names for them, as on the A4 invoice.

### Recently discharged patients

A new 'Recently discharged patients' view lists patients discharged from the current facility in the
past 24 hours, showing where and by whom each was discharged. Staff can find and follow up a
recently discharged patient without already knowing who they are.

**Key features**

- **Who is listed** - Inpatient, outpatient and emergency discharges at the facility in the past 24
  hours, including those the system makes automatically, such as outpatient encounters closed
  overnight. Each patient appears once, for their most recent discharge.
- **Table and search** - The table shows the patient's details, encounter type, area, department,
  discharging clinician and discharge date. It can be searched by patient, encounter type, area,
  department and discharging clinician, and clicking a row opens the patient.
- **Sidebar** - The view is listed as 'Recently discharged' under Facility admin, and a deployment
  can hide or move it in the sidebar layout settings.

## Patient Portal

### Patient Portal redesign and patient details

The Patient Portal has a new look, shared with the self check-in kiosk, and patients can now keep
their own details up to date. Patients see what they need to do, their upcoming appointments, their
details and their health record on one page, sized for a phone.

**Key features**

- **Redesigned dashboard** - 'To do' lists the forms the patient has been asked to complete, followed
  by upcoming appointments, the patient's details, and their health record. Ongoing conditions,
  allergies, medications, vaccinations and procedures each expand in place.
- **Editing their details** - Patients can change their contact details, address, emergency
  contact, insurance policy number and selected personal details such as title, ethnicity and
  occupation. Their name, date of birth, sex, patient ID and village cannot be changed through the
  portal.
- **Follows the deployment's patient fields** - Fields the deployment hides are hidden in the portal
  too, and fields it requires on 'Patient details' must be filled in before saving.
- **Changes apply straight away** - A patient's changes update their record immediately, without
  staff review, and reach facilities through sync like any other update. Changing the email on the
  record does not change the email the patient signs in with.
- **Forms** - Forms open page by page with a progress bar, check required answers before moving on,
  and warn before discarding unsubmitted answers.

**Security considerations**

A patient can only view and edit their own record, and the server enforces the same rules as the
portal, refusing changes to hidden fields and to anything outside the editable details. A form can
only be opened by the patient it was sent to while it is outstanding, and its response is always
recorded against that patient.

# 🔧 System enhancements

## Tamanu Desktop

- **'All patients' search** - The 'All patients' listing shows no patients until a search is run,
  with a 'Search for a patient' hint in their place. 'Search' and 'Clear' are available once a field
  is filled in, and the listing starts afresh each time it is opened.
- **Remembered filters and sorting** - The patient lists and worklists remember each user's search
  filters and column sort at each facility, including after logging out and on other devices.
  Searches for a single patient or request by name or ID are not kept.
- **Table pages** - Table footers read, for example, 'Showing 1–10 of 547'. The patient lists,
  worklists and admin panel tables show 50 or 100 rows per page, starting at 50, and remember each
  user's choice.
- **Patient lab results** - The patient's lab results are filtered by 'Test category' and by a 'Test
  or panel' field that picks several tests and panels at once. Results are grouped under each panel,
  followed by 'Individual tests'.
- **STAT medications** - Medications prescribed 'Immediately' carry a STAT label on the medication
  administration record and are listed first.
- **Every 2 hours frequency** - Medications can be prescribed 'Every 2 hours', with a dose in each of
  the day's twelve administration windows. Like other frequencies, it can be turned off for a
  deployment.
- **Deleting a procedure** - A procedure recorded in error can be deleted from its row's actions
  menu, along with any forms attached to it. Where the procedure was added to an in-progress invoice
  automatically, the invoice item is removed too. This needs permission to delete procedures.
- **Program registries** - A registry can require at least one related condition at registration.
  A new form question records a related condition and its category on the patient's registration,
  on desktop and mobile. 'Currently in' can be set when registering and changed when updating a
  registration, and the conditions column can be sorted.
- **QR code scanning** - Patient ID fields can be filled by scanning a QR code: on the new patient
  form, in 'Patient details' where the patient ID can be edited, and in the patient search on 'All
  patients', the inpatient, outpatient and emergency lists and program registries. Scans are checked
  against the deployment's patient ID format.
- **Insurance** - A patient's insurance is recorded as insurance plans on their invoices, so the
  'Insurer' field is removed from patient details, the new patient form, patient search and Patient
  Portal. 'Insurance policy number' stays. Payments from insurers are recorded against the invoice's
  insurance plan, and are imported through 'Insurance plan payments' in the admin panel.
- **Areas to be imaged** - On a new imaging request, 'Areas to be imaged' shows how many areas are
  chosen, and the imaging request lists each area on its own line.
- **Federated States of Micronesia death certificate** - The certificate prints on a single page.

## Tamanu Mobile

- **QR code search** - Patient search has a 'Scan QR code' button that finds a patient by their
  patient ID.
- **Screen kept awake during sync** - The screen stays on for the whole of every sync, whichever
  screen is showing, and returns to its normal timeout when the sync ends.
- **Storage** - Tamanu Mobile hands back unused storage in the background, so its database no
  longer grows indefinitely on the device.

## System administration

- **User profiles** - 'User profiles' shows the facilities each user can log in to, and can be
  filtered by facility.
- **Experimental AI** - AI features that send patient data to an external model are grouped under
  'Experimental AI' in settings, where only full administrators can change them. The AI form builder
  has its own switch and model. Existing AI settings are carried across. AI summaries leave out
  sensitive lab tests and sensitive medications, whoever generates them.
- **Setting up a facility server** - A new facility server shows its setting-up screen in place of
  the login screen until its first sync completes, and the setup wizard no longer logs the
  administrator in.
- **Browser performance telemetry** - Tamanu can measure how long pages and requests take in the
  browser, on Tamanu Desktop, the admin panel and Patient Portal, and keep the measurements on the
  server for analysis. It is off by default, and kept for a month once turned on.
- **FHIR resource identifiers** - FHIR resources created from Tamanu records get identifiers that
  are the same on every server, so an integration sees one identifier for a record wherever it
  reads it. Resources that already exist keep their identifiers.

## Security hardening

- **Server encryption key** - Each server must have its own encryption key. A deployment refuses the
  development key that ships with Tamanu and does not start without a key of its own. The upgrade
  re-encrypts secrets held under the development key once the server has its own.
- **Central authentication secrets** - The central server does not start unless both its
  authentication secrets are set, each at least 16 characters and not a few characters repeated.
- **Live updates** - Connections that carry live updates to Tamanu Desktop and Tamanu Mobile need a
  valid sign-in and end when it expires, and updates reach only the users and facilities they
  concern.
- **Facility sync server** - A facility server's internal sync service accepts connections only from
  the server itself, unless configured otherwise.
- **Reports** - A report's query is checked when a version is saved, without running it, and must
  be a single statement. Creating or importing a report that reads Tamanu's own tables, rather than
  the reporting schema, needs permission to change the report schema.
- **Form calculations** - Calculated questions and results use a fixed set of arithmetic,
  comparisons, conditions and maths functions. Importing a form rejects a calculation outside that
  set and names the part it does not support.
- **Uploads** - Uploaded files are limited to 50 MB, and attachments to their existing smaller limit.
- **Mobile sign-in** - Tamanu Mobile keeps each user's permissions separately, so offline sign-in
  uses the permissions from that user's own last online sign-in.

# 🐛 Tweaks and bug fixes

## Desktop

- **Search** - Typing an underscore or percent sign in a search no longer matches every record, and
  spaces around a patient search are ignored. A slow response to an earlier search no longer
  replaces the suggestions for what has since been typed. Choosing '(item not in list)' creates the
  new item once, including when chosen with the keyboard.
- **Encounters** - Clearing 'Referral source' in 'Edit encounter details' saves. A triage started
  from an appointment is linked to the appointment, and the patient's check-in options update
  straight away.
- **Labs and imaging** - Clearing 'Collected by', 'Specimen type' or 'Site' when editing a sample
  saves the cleared value. A printout of a request that is verified but not published no longer
  names the verifier as 'Published by'. 'View image (external link)' shows again on imaging requests
  with results in an external imaging system.
- **Medications** - 'Finalise all & print' no longer prints prescriptions that failed to save.
- **Invoicing** - An insured invoice that the patient has paid their share of in full shows as
  'Paid' rather than 'Partial'. Printed invoices follow the deployment's translations.
- **Vaccinations** - The COVID-19 vaccine certificate opens again.
- **Program registries** - The 'Currently in' filter combines with the other filters.
- **Charts and forms** - Long checkbox labels wrap rather than running into the next question.
- **Dashboard** - The tasks count loads at sites with many medication tasks, where it could time
  out.
- **Notes** - Logging out through inactivity with a note open no longer blocks the sidebar after
  logging back in.
- **Tooltips** - Long tooltips, such as a long list of facilities, no longer flicker.
- **AI form builder** - Form previews are checked against the same rules as saving, and corrected
  before they are shown, so saving a preview no longer fails unexpectedly.

## Mobile

- **Sync** - A failed sync shows the error's details where they are available, rather than 'Unknown
  error'.

## System

- **Certificates** - Servers no longer trust the emSign ECC Root CA - G3 certificate authority, which
  browsers are removing after its intermediate keys were found to be recoverable.

# ⚠️ Critical upgrade notes

### Required

**_Server encryption key_**

- Make sure every server has its own encryption key configured before upgrading. A server with no
  key, or using the development key that ships with Tamanu, will not start, and the upgrade stops
  and says how to provide one.
- Once a key is in place, the upgrade re-encrypts any secrets held under the development key, and
  names any configuration values it cannot decrypt so they can be re-encrypted.

**_Central authentication secrets_**

- Confirm both of the central server's authentication secrets are set, each at least 16 characters
  and not a few characters repeated. Without them the central server will not start.

**_Patient insurer_**

- The upgrade permanently removes each patient's recorded insurer, and the insurer reference data
  type. Export anything you need from it before upgrading, as it cannot be restored.
- An 'Insurer' field added to the 'All patients' search is removed.

**_Insurance plan payments_**

- Payment import spreadsheets identify the invoice's insurance plan rather than the insurer. Update
  any template your team uses.
- Insurer payments recorded before the upgrade are not matched to an insurance plan.

**_Form calculations_**

- Calculated questions and results that use anything outside the supported set of operations
  calculate no value after the upgrade. Review your forms' calculations, and re-import any form
  whose import now fails.

**_Mobile sign-in_**

- After upgrading, each user signs in to Tamanu Mobile online once before they can sign in offline
  on that device.

**_Facility sync server_**

- If anything outside a facility server's own host starts syncs on it, configure the address its
  sync service listens on, as it otherwise accepts connections only from the server itself.

**_Report authors_**

- Creating or importing reports that read Tamanu's own tables, rather than the reporting schema,
  needs permission to change the report schema. Give it to the roles that maintain these reports.

**_Syndromic surveillance_**

- If your deployment already has a syndromic surveillance program, check its program and form
  codes against the configuration guide. A program and form with the codes the feature uses become
  its syndromic surveillance form.

**_Patient Portal_**

- Patients using the portal can change their own contact, address, emergency contact, insurance and
  some personal details, and the changes apply to their record without staff review. Let staff know,
  so a changed phone number or address is not a surprise.
- Review which patient details your deployment requires and hides, as the portal follows the same
  settings.

**_All patients_**

- The 'All patients' listing no longer shows patients until a search is run. Let staff know before
  the upgrade.

### Optional

**_Self check-in kiosk_**

- Decide whether the kiosk suits any of your facilities. It is off until turned on for a facility.
- Set up an account for each kiosk tablet with only the permission to run the kiosk, and choose the
  forms patients complete for each appointment type and location booking type.

**_Syndromic surveillance_**

- Design your syndromic surveillance form and import it as a program, then decide whether it must
  be completed before a discharge is finalised.

**_Receipt printing_**

- Switch facilities that print invoices at a point-of-sale printer to the receipt layout. Every
  facility prints A4 until changed.

**_Recently discharged patients_**

- The view appears in the sidebar for every user who can list patients. Hide or move it in the
  sidebar layout settings if it does not suit your deployment.

**_Experimental AI_**

- Check that AI features you use are still turned on under 'Experimental AI' and the AI form
  builder's own settings.

**_Program registries_**

- Decide whether any registry should require a related condition at registration.

**_Browser performance telemetry_**

- Telemetry is off by default. Turn it on if you want to collect page and request timings for your
  deployment.

## Upgrade steps and recommended testing

### Required

**_Upgrade window_**

- Allow extra time for the central server's upgrade, which adds an index to its change log, usually
  its largest table.
- After upgrading, confirm the central server and every facility server start, and check the
  upgrade's output for configuration values it could not decrypt.

**_Form calculations_**

- Open forms that use calculated questions, such as a BMI calculation, and confirm the values still
  calculate.

**_Mobile_**

- Upgrade a Tamanu Mobile device, sign in online, then sign out and back in offline. Record some
  data and sync, and confirm the records appear on Tamanu Desktop.

**_Patient Portal_**

- Sign in to the portal as a test patient, change a contact detail and save. Confirm the change
  appears on the patient's record in Tamanu Desktop once the facility has synced.
