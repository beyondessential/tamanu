# Desktop

Guides to using Tamanu on a computer.

## Modules

- [Patients](patients/index.md): Finding, registering, and managing patient records.
- [Dashboard](dashboard/index.md): The overview a clinician lands on after signing in.
- [Encounters](encounters/index.md): Admitting, moving, and discharging patients.
- [Scheduling](scheduling/index.md): Outpatient appointments and location bookings.
- [Vitals](vitals/index.md): Recording and reviewing a patient's observations during an encounter.
- [Charts](charts/index.md): Recording and reviewing charted clinical data.
- [Tasks](tasks/index.md): Clinical tasks raised and completed during an encounter.
- [Notes](notes/index.md): Written clinical notes on an encounter.
- [Procedures](procedures/index.md): Procedures carried out during an encounter.
- [Medication](medication/index.md): Prescribing, dispensing, and administering medication.
- [Labs](labs/index.md): Requesting lab tests and reading results.
- [Imaging](imaging/index.md): Requesting imaging and reading results.
- [Forms](forms/index.md): Completing program forms and surveys for a patient.
- [Referrals](referrals/index.md): Referring a patient and tracking referrals.
- [Immunisation](immunisation/index.md): Recording vaccines and using the immunisation register.
- [Documents](documents/index.md): Files attached to a patient or an encounter.
- [Invoicing](invoicing/index.md): Invoices raised against an encounter.
- [Program registry](program-registry/index.md): Enrolling patients in program registries and tracking them.
- [Bed management](bed-management/index.md): Beds and locations across the facility.
- [Reports](reports/index.md): Running facility reports.
