Create a record for a patient who is not in Tamanu yet. Start from **All patients** under
**Patients** in the sidebar.

# Before you start

- Search for the patient first, so you don't create a second record for someone already in Tamanu.
  See [Find a patient](01-find-patient.md)
- To register a newborn with their birth details, see [Register a birth](03-register-birth.md)
- **Add new patient** works only if your account can create patient records. Contact your system
  administrator if you believe you should be able to register patients

# Steps

1. Select **Add new patient** at the top right of the **Patient listing**.

   The **Add new patient** window opens, with a new **National Health Number** at the top. To use a
   different number, select **Regenerate**.

   **[Screenshot: the Patient listing, with Add new patient outlined in red]**

2. Leave **Add new patient** selected at the top of the form.

3. Fill in **General information**.

   **First name**, **Last name**, **Date of birth** and **Sex** must be filled in, and are marked
   with a red asterisk. Your site may mark other fields too.

   **[Screenshot: the Add new patient window with General information filled in]**

4. Scroll down the window and fill in any other details you have.

   They sit under **Identification information**, **Contact information**, **Personal information**
   and **Location information**, and depend on how your site is set up. To link the patient to a
   parent who is already in Tamanu, search for them under **Mother** or **Father**.

5. Select **Create new patient**.

   The window closes and the new patient's record opens. See
   [View and edit a patient's details](04-view-and-edit-details.md).

   **[Screenshot: the new patient's record open on the Summary tab]**

# Respond to a possible duplicate

When you select **Create new patient**, Tamanu checks for existing patients with a similar name and
date of birth. If it finds any, the **Possible duplicate patient record** window opens. It lists them
under **Existing patient record/s**, above your **Proposed new patient record**.

1. Compare the existing records with the patient you are adding.

2. If the patient is listed, select their row.

   Their record opens, and the details you entered are discarded.

3. If the patient is not listed, select **Add new patient**.

   The new record is created and opens. To change the details you entered first, select **Back**.

   **[Screenshot: the Possible duplicate patient record window listing two existing records, with Add new patient outlined in red]**

# Cancel adding a patient

1. Select **Cancel**, or the cross at the top right of the window.

   The **Cancel new patient** window asks you to confirm.

2. Select **Cancel adding new patient** to discard the details you entered.

   To carry on with the patient instead, select **Back to adding new patient**.

   **[Screenshot: the Cancel new patient window]**
