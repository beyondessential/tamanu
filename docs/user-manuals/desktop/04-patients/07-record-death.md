Record that a patient has died, with the cause of death, and print their death certificate. Start
from the panel on the left of the patient's record.

**[Screenshot: Peter Waine's record, with Record death outlined in red]**

# Before you start

- Recording a death discharges the patient from any encounter they have open, and locks their record
  so it can no longer be changed. Make sure their current encounter is up to date first
- Once a death record is complete, it cannot be undone
- **Record death** shows only if your site records deaths in Tamanu and your account has
  permission. Contact your system administrator if you believe you should be able to record deaths

# Record a death

1. Select **Record death** at the bottom of the panel.

   The **Record patient death** window opens.

2. Fill in **Date & time of death** and **Attending clinician**.

   The current date and time, and your name, are filled in. Change them if needed.

   **[Screenshot: the first page of Record patient death, with Save and close and Continue outlined in red]**

3. If you know the cause of death, select **Continue**. If not, see
   [Save a death without its cause](#save-a-death-without-its-cause).

4. Fill in the cause of death.

   - **Cause of death**: the disease, injury or complication that caused the death, not the mode of
     dying such as heart failure. Rest your pointer on the **i** beside it for a reminder
   - **Time interval from onset to death**: a number, and its unit, such as hours or years
   - **Due to (or as a consequence of)**: the conditions that led to the cause of death, each with its
     time interval
   - **Other significant contributing condition**: other conditions that contributed. Select **Add
     additional** to add more
   - **Facility**: where the patient died. If they died elsewhere, tick **Died outside health
     facility** instead

   **[Screenshot: the cause of death page filled in, with Continue outlined in red]**

5. Select **Continue**, and answer the questions on each page that follows.

   The pages depend on the patient. They cover any recent surgery, whether an autopsy was requested,
   pregnancy where it applies, the birth for a baby under a year old, and the manner of death.

6. On the last page, select **Submit**.

   **Confirm death record** asks you to confirm.

   **[Screenshot: Confirm death record, with Record death outlined in red]**

7. Select **Record death**.

   The patient's record is locked. The **Summary** tab shows **Deceased**, with the location,
   clinician, underlying condition and date of death.

# Save a death without its cause

Record that a patient has died now, and add the cause of death later.

1. On the first page of **Record patient death**, select **Save and close**.

   **Patient will be auto-discharged and locked** asks you to confirm.

   **[Screenshot: Patient will be auto-discharged and locked, with Confirm outlined in red]**

2. Select **Confirm**.

   The patient's record is locked, and the **Summary** tab shows **Deceased**. **Death certificate
   pending** shows until the cause of death is added.

# Add the cause of death later

1. Select **Cause of death** in the panel.

   The **Record patient death** window opens at the cause of death.

   **[Screenshot: Cause of death and Revert death record in the panel, outlined in red]**

2. Carry on from step 4 of [Record a death](#record-a-death).

# Revert a death record

If a death was recorded in error, you can revert it until its cause of death has been added.

1. Select **Revert death record** at the bottom of the panel.

   The **Revert patient death** window opens. Reverting does not reopen any encounters that were
   closed when the death was recorded.

   **[Screenshot: the Revert patient death window, with Confirm outlined in red]**

2. Select **Confirm**.

   The patient's record is unlocked.

# View the death certificate

1. Select **View death certificate** on the **Summary** tab.

   The **Cause of death certificate** opens.

   **[Screenshot: the Summary tab of a deceased patient, with View death certificate outlined in red]**

2. Select **Print** to print it.

   **[Screenshot: the Cause of death certificate]**
