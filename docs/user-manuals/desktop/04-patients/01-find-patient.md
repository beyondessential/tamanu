Find a patient's record by searching for them, or by looking through the lists of patients in
hospital, at a clinic or in the emergency department. Start from **Patients** in the sidebar.

**[Screenshot: the Patient listing, with All patients under Patients in the sidebar outlined in red]**

# Before you start

The search fields depend on how your site is set up, so yours may differ from those described here.

# Search all patients

Search **All patients** to find anyone registered in Tamanu, whether or not they are visiting your
facility now. Search here before you register a new patient, so you don't create a second record for
someone already in Tamanu.

1. Select **Patients** in the sidebar, then **All patients**.

   The **Patient listing** opens. **Recently viewed** at the top lists the patients you have opened
   most recently. See [Move around a patient's record](../02-navigating-tamanu/02-move-around-patient-record.md).

2. Enter what you know about the patient under **Patient search**.

   - **NHN**: the patient's whole National Health Number. To scan it from the patient's QR code ID,
     select the QR code button beside the field and hold the code up to your camera
   - **First name** and **Last name**: part of a name is enough
   - **DOB**: the patient's date of birth

3. To search by other details, select the double arrow beside **Search**.

   More fields appear: **Cultural/traditional name**, **DOB from** and **DOB to** for a range of
   birth dates, **Sex** and **Village**. Tick **Include deceased patients** to include patients who
   have died.

4. Select **Search**.

   The patients who match are listed below the search. **Status** shows whether a patient is in
   hospital, at a clinic or in the emergency department now. A patient who has died is shown in red,
   with **Deceased** under **Status**.

   **[Screenshot: Patient search with the extra fields showing and Roy Antonini found, with the double arrow and Search outlined in red]**

   **[Screenshot: Patient search for Waine with Include deceased patients ticked, showing a deceased patient in red, with Include deceased patients outlined in red]**

5. Select the patient's row to open their record.

   To clear the search and start again, select **Clear**. To save the list as a spreadsheet, select
   **Download** below it.

# Sort a list

1. Select a column heading with an arrow beside it, such as **Last name**.

   The list is sorted by that column. Select the heading again to sort the other way.

# Find a patient in hospital

1. Select **Inpatients** under **Patients**.

   The **Admitted patient listing** lists the patients admitted to hospital at your facility, with
   the area and location they are in, their department, their clinician and their diet. **Recently
   viewed** shows only the patients you have opened who are in hospital.

2. To narrow the list, fill in **NHN**, **First name**, **Last name** or **Area**. To search by
   **Department**, **Clinician** or **Diet**, select the double arrow beside **Search** first.

3. Select **Search**.

   Your search stays in place when you leave the list and come back. Select **Clear** to see every
   patient again.

   **[Screenshot: the Admitted patient listing with Department, Clinician and Diet showing]**

4. Select the patient's row to open their record.

# Find a patient at a clinic

1. Select **Outpatients** under **Patients**.

   The **Outpatient listing** lists the patients with a clinic encounter open at your facility now.
   A patient booked for a clinic later is not listed until they are checked in.

   **[Screenshot: the Outpatient listing with patients listed]**

2. Search the list as you would **Inpatients**. **Diet** is not available here.

3. Select the patient's row to open their record.

# Find a patient in the emergency department

1. Select **Emergency patients** under **Patients**.

   The top of the page shows how many patients there are at each triage level, and how long they
   have waited on average. Below it, the patients still waiting to be seen are listed first, the most
   urgent at the top, then those who have waited longest. The colour of each patient's **Wait time**
   shows their triage level.

   **[Screenshot: Emergency patients, showing the patients at each triage level above the list]**

2. To narrow the list, fill in **Patient ID**, **First name**, **Last name** or **Clinician**. To
   search by **Triage category**, **Area** or **Location**, select the double arrow beside
   **Search** first. Then select **Search**.

   Your search stays in place when you leave the list and come back, until you select **Clear**.

3. Select the patient's row.

   The patient's emergency encounter opens. See [Encounters](../08-encounters/).
