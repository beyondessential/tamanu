A patient's record brings together who they are, their history and their visits to your facility.
Open it to check on a patient, and keep their details up to date. Start from a patient list under
**Patients** in the sidebar, or from **Recently viewed**.

**[Screenshot: Roy Antonini's record open on the Summary tab]**

# Open a patient's record

1. Find the patient and select their row. See [Find a patient](01-find-patient.md).

   The patient's record opens on the **Summary** tab.

2. Check the panel on the left.

   It shows the patient's name, sex, date of birth and age, and their **National Health Number**.
   Below these it lists their ongoing conditions, allergies, family history, other patient issues,
   care plans and program registries. See [Record a patient's history](05-record-history.md).

   If your site uses it, an **AI patient summary** sits above these lists. It is written by AI and
   may contain inaccuracies.

   The panel stays beside every page of the patient's record. To come back to the **Summary** tab
   from anywhere in the record, select the patient's name at the top of the panel.

3. Check the **Summary** tab.

   - The top shows the patient's current visit, with **View encounter** to open it. If they have no
     current visit, it shows **No current visit** and **Admit or check in**. See
     [Encounters](../08-encounters/)
   - If your site shows them, the patient's outpatient appointments and location bookings are next.
     See [Scheduling](../05-scheduling/)
   - **Encounter history** lists all the patient's visits, the most recent first. To narrow it,
     choose a **Type**, **Facility** or **Discharging clinician**. Select a visit to open it

4. Select another tab to see more of the patient's record.

   Which tabs you see, and their order, depend on your site and your permissions.

   | Tab | Covered in |
   | --- | --- |
   | **Details** | [Edit a patient's details](#edit-a-patients-details), below |
   | **Results** | [Labs](../17-labs/) |
   | **Referrals** | [Referrals](../19-referrals/) |
   | **Forms** | [Forms](../13-forms/) |
   | **Documents** | [Documents](../20-documents/) |
   | **Vaccines** | [Immunisations](../07-immunisations/) |
   | **Medication** | [Medications](../15-medications/) |
   | **Invoices** | [Invoicing](../21-invoicing/) |

# Sync a patient's full record

Your facility holds a patient's full record, including their visits, only once the record is synced
to it. Until then, **Sync patient records** shows on the **Summary** tab in place of **Encounter
history**.

1. Select **Sync patient records**.

   Tamanu starts bringing the patient's full record to your facility. While it does, **Patient is
   being synced, so records might not be fully updated.** shows above **Encounter history**.

   If your account cannot sync records, the **Summary** tab says the record is not marked for sync at
   your facility instead. Contact your system administrator if this patient's record should be
   synced.

# Edit a patient's details

1. Select the **Details** tab.

   The patient's details are shown in the same sections as when they were registered.

   **[Screenshot: the Details tab, with the Details tab outlined in red]**

2. Change the details you need.

   **First name**, **Last name**, **Date of birth** and **Sex** cannot be left empty.

3. Scroll to the bottom of the tab and select **Save**.

   **Patient details successfully updated** shows when the changes are saved.

   **[Screenshot: the bottom of the Details tab, with Save outlined in red]**
