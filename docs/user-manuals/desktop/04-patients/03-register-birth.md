Register a newborn baby, recording their birth details and linking them to their parents. Start
from **All patients** under **Patients** in the sidebar.

# Before you start

- Search for the baby first, in case they have already been registered. See
  [Find a patient](01-find-patient.md)
- To link the baby to their mother or father, the parent needs a record in Tamanu. If they don't have
  one, register them first. See [Register a new patient](02-register-new-patient.md)

# Steps

1. Select **Add new patient** at the top right of the **Patient listing**.

   The **Add new patient** window opens.

2. Select **Register birth** at the top of the form.

   A **Birth details** section appears in the form, and fields that don't apply to a newborn are
   hidden.

   **[Screenshot: the Add new patient window with Register birth selected and General information filled in, with Register birth outlined in red]**

3. Fill in **General information**.

   **First name**, **Last name**, **Date of birth** and **Sex** must be filled in.

4. Fill in **Birth details**.

   - **Time of birth** and **Gestational age (weeks)**
   - **Place of birth**. If the baby was born at a health facility, choose it under **Name of health
     facility (if applicable)**
   - **Attendant at birth** and **Name of attendant**
   - **Delivery type**, **Single/Plural birth** and **Birth order**
   - **Birth weight (kg)** and **Birth length (cm)**
   - The Apgar scores at 1, 5 and 10 minutes

   Your site may require some of these.

   **[Screenshot: the Birth details section filled in]**

5. Under **Personal information**, search for the baby's mother under **Mother** and their father
   under **Father**, then select the parent from the list.

   Each parent is listed with their National Health Number, sex and date of birth, so you can tell
   apart patients with the same name.

   **[Screenshot: Mother and Father, with the father selected]**

6. Fill in any other details you have, then select **Create new patient**.

   Tamanu checks for a possible duplicate, as it does for any new patient. See
   [Respond to a possible duplicate](02-register-new-patient.md#respond-to-a-possible-duplicate). The
   baby's record then opens. To print their birth notification, see
   [Print patient documents](06-print-patient-documents.md).
