Shortcuts and small tricks that save time in Tamanu Desktop. Each one works wherever you meet the
kind of field or screen it describes.

# Dates and times

- **Fill in the current date or time.** Select a date, time, or date and time field and type a
  lowercase **c**. The field fills in with today's date, and the current time where the field has
  one.
- **Use the calendar's buttons.** Open the calendar beside a date field and select **Today**, or
  **Now** in a time field, to fill it in. Select **Clear** to empty it.
- **Type a date instead.** Type the date straight into the field in the format it shows, such as
  dd/mm/yyyy, rather than picking it from the calendar. Tamanu reads it when you leave the field.
- **See a date in full.** Point at a date anywhere in Tamanu to see it written out with the day of
  the week, and the time where there is one.

# Searching

- **Search with part of a name or number.** The **NHN**, **First name** and **Last name** search
  fields find what you type anywhere in the value, in capitals or not. Typing `toni` in **Last name**
  finds Antonini.
- **Press Enter to search.** Press Enter in a search field instead of selecting **Search**.
- **Find a patient by an old ID.** On **All patients**, the **NHN** field also finds a patient by an
  older or secondary ID, when you type it in full.
- **Show more filters.** Select the double arrow beside **Search** to open **Advanced search**, which
  has extra filters such as **Include deceased patients**. Select it again to hide them.
- **Pick from a list.** In a field that suggests options as you type, select the field to see every
  option, type to narrow the list, and select the cross to clear it.
- **Add a value that isn't listed.** Some fields, such as an allergy's name, let you add a new
  value. Type it, then select the option ending in **(item not in list)**. This needs permission, so
  contact your system administrator if you believe you should be able to.

# Lists and tables

- **Read text that is cut short.** Point at text ending in an ellipsis (…) to read all of it.
- **Download more than one page.** **Download** under a table saves only the rows on the current
  page. To save more, raise the number of rows shown at the bottom of the table first.

# Encounters

- **Put the tabs in your order.** Drag an encounter's tabs, such as **Vitals** and **Notes**, into
  the order you use them. Tamanu saves the order to your account, so it is the same in every
  encounter, at every facility.
- **Check a reading against its normal range.** In **Vitals** and **Charts**, values outside the
  normal range are highlighted. Point at one to see the range it falls outside, or point at a
  measure's name to see its normal range. Ranges show only where your site has set them.
- **Spot edited values.** An asterisk (\*) after a value means it has been edited since it was
  recorded.
- **Act on a task quickly.** On the **Tasks** tab, point at a task to show buttons to
  **Mark as complete**, **Mark as not complete**, **Mark as to-do** or **Delete** it. Point at a
  task's name or status to see who requested or completed it, and when.
- **Update several tasks at once.** Tick the tasks to select them. Only tasks with the same status
  can be selected together.

# Notes

- **Keep the record in view.** The note window sits over the patient's record without hiding it, so
  you can scroll and read the record while you write. Drag the window's title bar to move it, or
  drag its edges to resize it.
- **Close a note with Escape.** Press Escape to close the note window. Tamanu opens **Discard note**
  to check before it removes anything you have written.
- **Finish the note before moving on.** You cannot leave the patient's record while a note is open.
  See [Move around a patient's record](02-move-around-patient-record.md).

# Scheduling

- **Move between days with the arrow keys.** On **Outpatient appointments**, and **Location
  bookings** in **Daily** view, select a day in the strip of days, then press the left or right
  arrow key to move a day at a time.
- **Jump to a month.** Type a month and year, such as `March 2027`, in the month box above the
  calendar and press Enter.
- **Book from the calendar.** On **Location bookings**, select an empty space in the calendar to
  start a booking with that location and date filled in. In **Daily** view the time is filled in
  too.
- **Move a booking.** On **Location bookings** in **Daily** view, drag a booking up or down to move
  it to another time in the same location.
- **Book several time slots.** When booking a location, select the first time slot, then the last.
  Tamanu books every slot between them.
- **See a booking in full.** Point at a booking or appointment to see its full time and patient
  name, which may be cut short on the calendar.

# Windows and messages

- **Close a window with Escape.** Press Escape to close most windows. Selecting outside a window
  does not close it, so you cannot lose your work with a stray click.
- **Dismiss a message.** Select a message that pops up to dismiss it, or point at it to keep it on
  screen.

# What Tamanu remembers

- **Saved to your account:** your encounter tab order, the chart you last chose on the **Charts**
  tab, and which measures you chose to show together in a graph.
- **Saved for each facility:** your filters on **Location bookings**, **Outpatient appointments** and
  the **Dashboard**'s **Upcoming tasks**.
- **Kept until you log out:** the search terms on the patient, lab, imaging and medication lists,
  while you move around Tamanu. Reloading the page clears them too.
- **Reset each time:** the number of rows a table shows, the order you sort it in, and whether the
  sidebar is collapsed.

# Good to know

- **Use one browser tab.** Tamanu works in one browser tab at a time. If you open it in a second
  tab, it tells you to continue in the tab you already have open.
- **Pasting may be turned off.** Your site can stop text being pasted into most fields.
